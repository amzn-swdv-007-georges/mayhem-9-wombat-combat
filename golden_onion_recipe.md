# Sizzling Sausage Sandwich

## Ingredients

* 1 sausage finger
* 1 bread roll
* Caramelised onions
* 1 slice cheddar cheese
* Tomato sauce
* Mustard

## Method

1. Cook the sausage until golden and sizzling.
2. Lightly toast the bread roll.
3. Add the sausage, caramelised onions, and cheese.
4. Top with tomato sauce and a little mustard.
5. Serve hot.
